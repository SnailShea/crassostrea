TLS versions

TLS 1.2 and 1.3 are always accepted. Turn on TLS 1.1 or 1.0 only for clients that cannot use anything newer: they are insecure, and accepting them also relaxes the OpenSSL policy of that service (security level 0, SHA-1 signatures). Sending servers that fail the TLS handshake usually retry without TLS. SSL 3.0 is not available: OpenSSL 3 no longer includes it.
